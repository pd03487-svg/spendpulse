## 1. Getting Started

When you launch SpendPulse for the first time, the system comes pre-loaded with a **realistic 4-month sample dataset**. This allows you to explore the charts, budget alerts, and export reports immediately.

- **Reset / Reload Demo Data**: Go to **Settings** > click **Load Demo Dataset**.
- **Start Fresh**: Go to **Settings** > **Danger Zone** > click **Erase Transactions**.

## 2. Recording Transactions

SpendPulse supports dual-entry tracking for both **Income** (revenue, salary, dividends, freelance) and **Expenses** (rent, groceries, dining, utilities, subscriptions).

### Adding a Transaction:
1. Click the **+ New Transaction** button in the sidebar or top header (or use the **Quick Add** bar on the dashboard).
2. Choose **Expense** or **Income**.
3. Enter the numeric **Amount** and **Title / Description**.
4. Select a **Category** from the dropdown.
5. Set the **Date**, **Payment Method**, and optional **Tags** (e.g. `Tax, Client, Trip`).
6. Toggle **Recurring** if this is a repeated monthly charge or salary deposit.
7. Click **Save Record**.

### Filtering & Searching Records:
On the **Transactions** page, you can:
- **Search by keyword**: Instant real-time filtering across titles, tags, and memos.
- **Filter by Type**: Show all, income-only, or expense-only.
- **Filter by Category & Date**: Filter by current month, last month, or custom date ranges.
- **Bulk Actions**: Select multiple checkboxes to batch delete or export selected rows to CSV.

## 3. Managing Categories & Budgets

Setting spending targets is essential for maintaining a healthy savings rate (>20%).

### Setting Category Budgets:
1. Navigate to **Budgets & Categories**.
2. Review the **Overall Monthly Budget Health** card at the top.
3. Locate the category you wish to limit (e.g., *Groceries* or *Dining Out*).
4. Click the **Edit (pencil)** icon on the card, input your target monthly budget limit, and click **Save**.
5. The progress bar will automatically update:
   - **Green (Normal)**: Spending is below 80% of budget.
   - **Amber (Warning)**: Spending is between 80% and 100% of budget.
   - **Red (Exceeded)**: Spending has exceeded the monthly budget limit.

### Creating Custom Categories:
1. On the **Budgets** page, click **New Category**.
2. Select whether it's an **Expense** or **Income** category.
3. Pick a custom name, choose from 25+ icons, and pick an accent color.
4. Set an optional monthly budget cap and save.

## 4. Exploring Analytics & Monthly Charts

Navigate to **Analytics & Charts** to view visual intelligence powered by Recharts:

- **Monthly Cash Flow Growth (Area Chart)**: Displays your income versus expense velocity over the past 3 to 12 months.
- **Category Proportions (Donut Chart)**: Interactive breakdown of which sectors consume the largest percentage of your budget.
- **Day-of-Month Spending Heatmap (Bar Chart)**: Analyzes spending across days 1 through 31 to identify peak billing spikes.
- **Budget vs. Actual Comparison (Bar Chart)**: Compares actual outflow against category limits side-by-side.

## 5. Generating & Exporting Reports (PDF & CSV)

SpendPulse features a built-in **Report Studio** for financial auditing and tax filings:

### Generating an Official PDF Statement:
1. Go to **Export Reports**.
2. Choose your desired time window (e.g., *Current Calendar Month*, *This Year*, or *Custom Date Range*).
3. Review the live statement document preview.
4. Click **Download PDF Statement**. The system will generate and download an official formatted PDF with summary metrics and an itemized ledger.

### Exporting CSV & JSON:
- Click **Export CSV** to download a spreadsheet compatible with Microsoft Excel, Apple Numbers, and Google Sheets.
- Click **JSON Backup** to download a complete raw database backup.
- Click **Print** to use browser-optimized print formatting.

## 6. Multi-Currency & Theme Customization

- **Change Active Currency**: Click the currency button in the top header or navigate to **Settings** to choose between `USD ($)`, `EUR (€)`, `GBP (£)`, `INR (₹)`, `JPY (¥)`, `CAD (CA$)`, `AUD (AU$)`, and `SGD (S$)`.
- **Toggle Themes**: Click the Sun/Moon icon in the top header to toggle between Dark Slate and Clean Light mode.

---

## 7. Backups, Restores & Data Privacy

SpendPulse is designed with an **offline-first security model**:
- Your financial data resides securely in your local browser storage.
- You can create full portable backups at any time by exporting the JSON file in **Settings**.
- To restore a backup on another device or browser, click **Choose Backup File** in Settings.
